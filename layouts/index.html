Prof. Carlo Cenedese / Prof. Shengling Shi

DCSC Colloquia is the lunch-time talk series of the Delft Center for Systems and Control (DCSC) at TU Delft, alongside a companion strand of online seminars. Speakers range from PhD students to faculty, covering the group's research in systems and control broadly. This site archives past talks with their recordings and lists upcoming ones.

We welcome proposals for both formats, on any topic in systems and control.
